# External human ASPA activity and amount

Frozen exploratory transfer rule for an independent enzyme. The human ASPA abundance head was previously frozen and trained on G6PD. Use its predicted within-panel abundance percentile and the distance of the changed residue to active-site ligands in the independent deposited structure, 2O4H (chain A zinc and tetrahedral reaction-intermediate analogue). Require an exact canonical sequence match or a fully verified constant-offset sequence alignment covering each tested residue. Use minimum nonhydrogen atom distance, ignoring altlocs except first/single conformers. Use a conventional contact distance of 5 Å. The categorical rule predicts both effects low for predicted abundance below the panel median. Otherwise it predicts selective activity loss at contacting residues and neither effect elsewhere. The median is an exploratory rule for a rank output, not a calibrated WT threshold. The abundance-only control has the same rules with contact disabled. No coefficients, assay measurements, ClinVar calls or EVEE scores enter this rule. Test the whole available ASPA scored panel without adapting any threshold. Freeze all available ASPA predictions before transcribing and joining paired labels. Initial source discovery disclosed a few results and the early study's amount-only audit is already exposed. This study is retrospective.

Primary evaluation is Hershfield et al. (2007), one independent COS-7 lysate experiment with a direct radiometric substrate-hydrolysis assay and separately measured protein on immunoblots. Include all individually identified single-substitution human ASPA constructs with an explicit abundance state relative to WT and an explicit undetectable, absent or WT-like activity result in the authors' text or figure captions. Censor intermediate or contradictory findings within the same study, including lysate amount versus normalized specific activity where they differ. No densitometry or arbitrary estimates from figure pixels. WT-like abundance and activity within the reported WT range are observed categories. A qualitative decrease, strong reduction or absence of protein constitutes low abundance. Stratify selective versus dual loss among the measured activity-loss cases. Treat a prediction of neither as a miss for those classes. Separately report three-way confusion including unchanged controls, class counts and arithmetic macro recall. Sentences of the source can expose identities before outcome transcription. Variants are not assumed independent.

Secondary check in a different human-cell system (Mendes et al. 2017). Use all reported individual single substitutions, score activity lower than 10% of WT as low, above 50% as preserved, censor between, and accept the original immunoblot interpretation. Keep all results from this measurement system separate; no pooled comparison or claim of clinical mechanism. An absence of reduced abundance in this study can only test specificity for the dual-loss category and sensitivity for selective loss. Report inconsistent results between experiments if a substitution overlaps.
